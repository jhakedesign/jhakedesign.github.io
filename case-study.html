Project
CREATE

A design-education curriculum built with the ease to learn video production and graphic design principles along with fun and opportunity for creativity.

Role
Creative Manager
Timeline
2017 – 2020
Client
PerXuade / XP Synergy
Deliverables
Curriculum, Branding, Print

Overview

XP Synergy and PerXuade needed a summer program that could take students with zero design background and get them producing professional level work within a month. I rebuilt the curriculum from the syllabus up, pairing every lesson with the idea of running their own creative team. Along with this object the students utilized the lessons in Photoshop and Premiere to build print and video assets for XP Synergy Opportunity Now program.
